How to play

  • Aim in the strip outside the grid and release to drop a fruit
  • Two matching fruits merge into the next tier
  • When Next shows ✥, you must swipe: gravity turns that way, 2048 tiles slide, and a new tile appears
  • You can't drop into a column whose top cell holds a tile
  • Tiles push a little fruit; if too much is in the way they bounce back
  • A 32 tile is hollow — roll a Tier 7 fruit into it and both burst
  • Fruit poking past the dashed line for 2 seconds ends the game
  • Goals: make 64, fill a 32, make Tier 8 — one star each
  • Keys: A / D aim · Space drop · arrows swipe
